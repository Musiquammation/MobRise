**App name** (≤ 30): `MobRise: Crowd Runner`

**Short description** (≤ 80):
> Grow your crowd, dodge the traps and fire your runners from a cannon. No ads.

**Full description:**

> You start with a handful of runners. The goal is to reach the end of the level with the biggest crowd you can. Slide your finger left or right to steer, and the rest comes down to the choices you make.
>
> GATES
> Gates along the track change the size of your crowd: x3, +50, -30%, or a fixed number of runners. You have to pick fast, and the gate that looks best is not always the right one.
>
> OBSTACLES
> Saws, spikes and spike balls take out every runner they touch. Archers shoot where you are going, not where you are, so you need to change direction at the right moment. Fans push your crowd to the side. Over the gaps, use a jump pad or a speed strip if you don't want to lose anyone.
>
> FIGHTS
> When you meet the red army, the two crowds crash into each other and the bigger one wins. Some levels end with a boss whose sword sweeps across the track. Surround him with enough runners before he clears the way. Gray runners standing on the road join you as soon as you touch them.
>
> THE CANNON
> At the end of each level, your crowd climbs into a cannon. Aim at the mystery boxes: every box you hit gives you coins.
>
> SKINS
> Coins buy skins, and each skin changes the way you play. There are four stats:
> - Flight: your runners jump on their own when the ground ends.
> - Shield: some of your runners carry a shield that blocks one hit.
> - Speed: your crowd runs faster.
> - Coins: you earn more coins when you win a level.
>
> The Ninja is fast, the Viking is hard to break, the King can double your earnings, and the Wizard and the Angel fly over the gaps. Some levels get a lot easier with the right skin.
>
> NO ADS
> There are no ads in the game: nothing between levels, and no videos to watch for a bonus. There are no in-app purchases either. Everything is unlocked by playing.
>
> Each level adds something new, and you can replay the ones you have already beaten to earn more coins.
