# Google Play listing graphics: requirements

From the Play Console "Main store listing" page:

- **App icon**: PNG or JPEG, ≤ 1 MB, exactly 512×512 px. Play applies its own mask (rounded square), so keep the subject away from the corners.
- **Feature graphic**: PNG or JPEG, ≤ 15 MB, exactly 1024×500 px. It is shown when the app is featured, sometimes with Play UI on top: keep the logo and text in the left two thirds.
- **Phone screenshots**: 2 to 8 images. PNG or JPEG, ≤ 8 MB each, 16:9 or 9:16, each side between 320 and 3840 px. To be eligible for promotion: at least 4 screenshots, 3 of them 16:9 or 9:16 at a minimum of 1080 px.
- **7" tablet screenshots**: up to 8, same rules as phone (sides 320–3840 px).
- **10" tablet screenshots**: up to 8, 16:9 or 9:16, sides between 1080 and 7680 px.
- At least two phone or tablet screenshots in total.
- Translations without localized graphics reuse the default language's graphics.

Text limits: app name ≤ 30 characters, short description ≤ 80, full description ≤ 4000.
