# Sounds and music

## Music

Played one after the other in a loop, with a 2.5 s crossfade (PLAYLIST in src/audio/audio.ts).

- `music.mp3`: "Riverside Ride" (long loop) by **Matthew Pablo** — http://www.matthewpablo.com
  License: **CC-BY 3.0: attribution required**, shown in the in-game settings (`settings.credits` in src/i18n/locales).
  Source: https://opengameart.org/content/riverside-ride
- `music2.mp3`: "Children's March Theme" by cleytonkauffman — CC0 (public domain)
  Source: https://opengameart.org/content/childrens-march-theme (loudness matched to music.mp3)
