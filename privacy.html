Privacy Policy – MobRise

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy applies to the MobRise mobile game (package com.musiquammation.mobrise), developed by Musiquammation.

Data collection

MobRise does not collect any personal or sensitive user or device data. No account or sign-in is required.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (completed levels, coins, unlocked skins and settings) is saved locally on your device only. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

Data sharing and third parties

MobRise does not share any data with third parties. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.

Children

Since no personal data is collected, MobRise is safe for users of all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
